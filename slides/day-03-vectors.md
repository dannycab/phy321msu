$$\mathbf{r} = r_x\hat{x} + r_y\hat{y} + r_z\hat{z}$$
$$\mathbf{r} = r_x\hat{i} + r_y\hat{j} + r_z\hat{k}$$
$$\mathbf{r} = r_x\hat{e}_1 + r_y\hat{e}_2 + r_z\hat{e}_3$$
$$\mathbf{r} = \langle r_x, r_y, r_z\rangle$$
