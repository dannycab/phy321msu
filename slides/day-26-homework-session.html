Duffing Equation#

\[\ddot{x} + \beta \dot{x} + \alpha x + \gamma x^3 = F_0 \cos(\omega t)\]

Lorenz System#

\[\dot{x} = \sigma (y - x)\]
\[\dot{y} = x (\rho - z) - y\]
\[\dot{z} = x y - \beta z\]
